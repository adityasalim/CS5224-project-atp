Provider participation rules

Version 1 — 1 November 2026

  • Providers supply accurate identity, availability and pricing.
  • Listings clearly state inclusions, exclusions and whether flights are included.
  • Profiles and listings require administrator approval before recommendation.
  • The registered contact email is shown to travellers for enquiries.
  • No payments or bookings occur through this platform. Misleading listings may be suspended.

Listings labelled TEST ONLY are synthetic fixtures, not real offers.
